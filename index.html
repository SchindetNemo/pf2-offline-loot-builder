Loot

    Add items from the table or roll random loot.

    Builds random runed gear from the mundane base weapons and armor: fundamental runes by level, plus property runes (one per potency bonus). Results use the party level window above and go straight into the loot list; click “Random loot” to roll.

    Only property runes that fit the base (melee, thrown, bludgeoning, light/heavy armor…) are used. Runes limited to metal or non-metal armor and the few restricted-to-trait runes are skipped.

    Foundry macro

    In Foundry: Macro Directory → Create Macro → Type Script → paste → Execute (as GM). Creates a loot actor in Actors > Loot with these items, scrolls, wands and coins, and whispers a summary to the GM.

    Owned items

    1. In Foundry run the macro below as GM: it reads the characters in your Party actor (or an actor folder named “The Party”) and copies a list of their permanent items to the clipboard. 2. Paste that list into the box and press Import. Those items are then left out of random loot and marked in the table. Scrolls, wands and consumables are ignored.
